What is it

An economics competition with a real client.

The TANK Econ Games is open to all NKU students. Teams receive a real data set and a set of problems from our case partner, then build a recommendation that tackles one of them.

It runs on The Econ Games competition model: a data drop, a hard deadline, short pitches, and a live final on the main stage. You don't need to be an economics major. Teams with mixed skills tend to do well.

Data and case partner: Transit Authority of Northern Kentucky. TANK runs public bus service across Boone, Campbell, and Kenton counties. The problems you'll work on come straight from them.

The problems

Pick one of three tracks.

TANK's problem statements are released at kickoff. Each team picks one problem to address in its final presentation.

DData

Read the numbers

Dig into ridership, operations, or performance data and turn what you find into evidence TANK can act on.

BBusiness

Make the call

Build an operational or strategic recommendation that makes the case for what TANK should do next, and why.

SSocial

Serve the community

Design a community- or equity-focused service solution that changes how riders across Northern Kentucky experience transit.

Specific problem statements stay sealed until the 10:00 AM data drop on February 4.

FAQ

Questions.

Who can compete?

Any NKU student. You don't need to be an economics or business major.

How big is a team?

Two to five students. Up to 30 teams can compete.

What can we win?

$3,000 for first place, $2,000 for second, and $1,000 for third.

What will we be working on?

A real data set from the Transit Authority of Northern Kentucky (TANK). At kickoff, TANK releases several problems across data, business, and social tracks. Your team picks one and builds a recommendation around it.

How long do we have?

The work period starts at 11:00 AM Thursday, February 4, and runs until the 2:00 PM submission deadline on Friday, February 5.

How long is the presentation?

Eight minutes in Round 1. If your team wins its room, you present again in the final round on the main stage at 3:30 PM.

Where is it?

Venue and room assignments are being finalized and will be posted here.

How do teams register?

Register through NKU's registration page. Early bird registration ends December 1, and space is limited to 30 teams.

Who do I contact with questions?
