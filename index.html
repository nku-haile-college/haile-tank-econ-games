What is it

An economics competition with a real client.

Analyze the data. Pick a problem. Pitch the fix.

The TANK Econ Games is a two-day applied economics competition open to all NKU students. Teams receive a real data set and a set of problems from our case partner, then spend the work period building a recommendation that tackles one of them.

It runs on The Econ Games competition model, so the format is proven: a fixed data drop, a hard submission deadline, short pitches, and a live final on the main stage.

You don't need to be an economics major. TANK's problems span data analysis, business strategy, and community service design, so teams with mixed skills tend to do well.

Data and case partner: Transit Authority of Northern Kentucky. TANK runs public bus service across Boone, Campbell, and Kenton counties. The problems you'll work on come straight from them.

Choose your route

Three kinds of problems.

TANK's problem statements are released at kickoff. They fall into three tracks, and each team picks one problem to address in its final presentation.

Questions

Before you board.

Who can compete?

Any NKU student. You don't need to be an economics or business major.

How big is a team?

Two to five students. Up to 30 teams can compete.

What can we win?

$3,000 for first place, $2,000 for second, and $1,000 for third.

What will we be working on?

A real data set from the Transit Authority of Northern Kentucky (TANK). At kickoff, TANK releases several problems across data, business, and social tracks. Your team picks one and builds a recommendation around it.

How long do we have?

The work period starts at 11:00 AM Thursday, February 4, and runs until the 2:00 PM submission deadline on Friday, February 5.

How long is the presentation?

Eight minutes in Round 1. If your team wins its room, you present again in the final round on the main stage at 3:30 PM.

Where is it?

Venue and room assignments are being finalized and will be posted here.

How do teams register?

Register through NKU's registration page. Early bird registration ends December 1, and space is limited to 30 teams.

Who do I contact with questions?

Dr. Abdullah Al-Bahrani at albahrania1@nku.edu.

What is The Econ Games?

A competition format where student teams analyze a partner organization's real data and present recommendations to judges under a tight deadline. The TANK Econ Games is built on that model.
